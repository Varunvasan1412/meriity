## Platform

web

## Users
Owners and decision-makers of Melbourne / Australian small-to-mid businesses where trust decides the sale: legal firms, healthcare & clinics, accounting & tax, finance & mortgage brokers, wealth advisers, NDIS & care providers, education & tutoring, trades & home services, events & hospitality, professional services, e-commerce. They arrive suspecting their marketing isn't converting and are evaluating whether Meriity is a credible partner worth a call.

## Product Purpose
Meriity's company website. It must convince a visitor that they lose business on trust (not price), and move them to request a free **Trust Check** or **book a Growth Call**. Success = qualified enquiries. Later the site will connect to a CRM / admin panel (leads already stored in MySQL via the Express API).

## Positioning
"Make trust visible." A Melbourne, founder-led growth partner (Deepesh Edappara, Melbourne Business School alumnus) that fixes foundations before spending on ads, via a six-stage Growth Framework: Assess → Establish Foundations → Strategise → Execute → Report & Refine → Scale & Sustain. Frames marketing around five trust touchpoints: Searched, Reviewed, Followed, Visited, Recognised.

## Operating Context
Visitors compare agencies online, often on mobile, before ever calling. Content source of truth: client PDF "Meriity home page (desktop)" (content only — its layout/visual design is NOT binding).
